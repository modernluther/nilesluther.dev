## Problem

The Monet and Venice exhibition needed a 36‑minute symphony played in immersive audio, in a gallery where the system had to stay hidden beside the paintings.

## What I built

A 4.1.4 Dolby Atmos installation for the Brooklyn Museum. I designed the signal flow with the museum's architects and Genelec's engineers and calibrated the system in GLM.
