## Problem

A brand under a misinformation campaign needs to know what is being said about it, by whom, and why. The evidence sits in thousands of raw comments under a single video.

## What I built

An LLM pipeline that turns a video's raw comment stream into brand reports for Creative Concepts, a marketing and PR consultancy. The model semantically analyzes each comment for narrative and intent, and every row keeps its source.
