## Problem

Five compositions, recorded with nine session musicians, needed a storefront that paid the musicians their share and let anyone buy without a crypto wallet.

## What I built

A storefront in WordPress with custom HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, selling ERC-721 editions on Optimism through Manifold.
